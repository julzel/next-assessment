# Task: Brand Blueprint Builder

## Phase 1

A page builder where a user can:

1. Capture the client's brand direction - a guided flow that surfaces things like visual
   style, color and typography direction, tone of voice, and brand personality.
2. Turn answers into a Brand Blueprint - a clear, presentable output our team can hand
   off or reference: think a structured summary/one-pager, not a raw dump of form answers.
3. See it come together as they go - the client (or the team member running the session)
   should get some live sense of the blueprint forming, not just a black-box result at the
   end.
4. Save and revisit - a client's inputs and resulting blueprint should persist, so it can be
   picked up again pre-onboarding and referenced during onboarding.

## Phase 2

Add AI-assisted editing to the Blueprint: after the initial capture flow generates a first draft,
the client can describe a change in plain language - "make the tone more playful," "swap the color
direction to something warmer," "this doesn't sound like us, we're more minimal than bold" - and 
the Blueprint updates accordingly.
